In short

A small learned network degrades the diffusion model's prediction directly in latent space. PASEO conditions on it with no back-propagation through the diffusion network.

Fast latent solvers handle only a fixed set of degradations. General ones are slow, because every step differentiates through the denoiser. PASEO keeps the generality and drops that cost: one solver covers super-resolution, deblurring, inpainting, JPEG restoration and nonlinear blur.

The idea / five equations

01 / The gradient problem

xt sits inside the denoiser: every step back-propagates through it.

02 / The way out: a proxy

Noise prediction at a proxy state, as in DING: it becomes a constant.

03 / A learned operator is not Gaussian

The operator is a neural network, so the posterior has no closed form.

04 / Linearize the operator, repeatedly

Only Jacobian-vector products are needed, never the Jacobian itself.

05 / Perturb, then solve

Kj Gaussian posterior gain  ·  cj linearization offset

Noise on the prior mean and the measurement, then one linear solve: a posterior sample for the price of the mean.
